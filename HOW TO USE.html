StudyFlow – How to use

A simple guide for people who aren't programmers. The app itself is in Hebrew; the Hebrew words below in colored boxes are the exact words you'll see on screen.

1 What is it?

StudyFlow is a day planner for students. You enter your class timetable once. After that, you just tell it what you need to do – for example מחר ללמוד שעתיים סטטיסטיקה ולהתאמן ("tomorrow, study statistics for two hours and work out") – and it finds free time around your classes, without ever putting two things at the same time.

🔒 No AI and no server. Everything is saved only in your browser, on this device. If you use the microphone, the browser itself (Google or Apple) turns your speech into text.

2 How to open it

  • Online: open the StudyFlow website in Chrome, Edge or Safari. On a phone you can add it to the home screen and use it like an app.
  • On this computer: double-click Open StudyFlow Handmade. A black window appears – keep it open while you use the app. Your browser opens at http://127.0.0.1:4174. It needs the free program Node.js (nodejs.org, "LTS").

Each address keeps its own data. The website, and the copy on your computer, do not share what you entered. To move data, use a backup (section 6).

3 The five screens

  • היום Today – the box ספר לי מה אתה צריך לעשות ("tell me what you need to do"), your day hour by hour, and your progress.
  • לימודים Studies – your class timetable and your courses. Each course has its own page: syllabus, topics, exams and progress.
  • עבודה Work – your workplaces and hourly wage, and your shifts (night shifts and weekly shifts too). Shifts go into your schedule, and each month shows your hours and an estimated pay, before tax.
  • מטרות אישיות Personal goals – sport and hobbies with a simple target, like 3 workouts a week or 300 pages.
  • יומן Calendar – the whole week at a glance.

4 First time: enter your timetable

  1. Go to לימודים.
  2. Fastest: many university portals and Google Calendar can export your timetable as a calendar file (.ics). Click ייבוא מקובץ יומן and choose the file. Check the list and confirm.
  3. Or by hand: click שיעור, type the course name, pick the days and the hours.
  4. Open a course and click סילבוס / נושאים to load the syllabus (a Word .docx or text file) or paste the list of topics. Lines with an exam or submission and a date become deadlines.

5 Every day

  1. On היום, write (or say, with the 🎤 button) what you need to do, and click לסדר לי.
  2. A card shows what was understood: each item, its day, and the time found for it. You can change anything.
  3. If something collides with a class or another plan, it is marked, and free times are offered instead. Nothing is saved until you click להכניס ללו״ז.
  4. When you finish something, click סיימתי. Study time counts toward the course, and workouts toward the goal.
  5. On a course or goal page, מצא לי זמן ("find me time") lists free slots in the coming week. Tap one to add it.

After any change, the message at the bottom has לבטל את השינוי to undo it.

6 Backup and moving to another device

Open העדפות (settings) → גיבוי והעברה למכשיר אחר. Download a backup to save a file; on the other device, load that file. Clearing the browser's data deletes what you entered, so download a backup from time to time.

7 Problems?

  • The microphone doesn't work: allow microphone access in the browser. Speech recognition needs an internet connection and works in Chrome, Edge and Safari.
  • A white page when opening index.html directly: use the website or Open StudyFlow Handmade instead.
  • "Windows protected your PC" when opening the launcher: click More info → Run anyway.
